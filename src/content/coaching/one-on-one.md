## Your personal path to better health

The 1:1 coaching is for women who want not just knowledge, but personal guidance. Over 12 weeks, we work together on your individual health goals — whether hormonal balance, cycle-aware training, or sustainable dietary changes.

### How I work

My approach is always evidence-based and holistic. I don't just look at individual symptoms, but at the interplay of hormones, training, nutrition, and lifestyle. Every plan is tailored to your personal situation — no one-size-fits-all solutions.

### Who is coaching for?

- You want to bring your hormones into balance
- You want to align your training with your cycle
- You're looking for a sustainable nutrition strategy
- You want professional, medical guidance on your journey
