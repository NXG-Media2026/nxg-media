## Why phase-based nutrition makes sense

Your body has different needs in each cycle phase. During the follicular phase, estrogen levels rise — your body needs different nutrients than in the luteal phase when progesterone dominates.

With this checklist, you have the most important recommendations at a glance — evidence-based and practical.

## What the checklist covers

### Menstrual phase (Days 1–5)
Focus on iron, omega-3 fatty acids, and anti-inflammatory foods.

### Follicular phase (Days 6–13)
Light, protein-rich nutrition with plenty of fresh vegetables and fermented foods.

### Ovulation phase (Days 14–16)
Antioxidants and fiber for the hormonal peak.

### Luteal phase (Days 17–28)
Complex carbohydrates, magnesium, and B vitamins to combat PMS symptoms.
