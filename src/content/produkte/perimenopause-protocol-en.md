## Perimenopause — when everything changes

Your body is changing, and you're not sure why. Your cycle becomes irregular, you sleep worse, gain weight, and don't feel like yourself anymore. Welcome to perimenopause — the phase nobody talks about.

### Do you recognize yourself?

- **Hot flashes and night sweats** — sudden and unexpected
- **Sleep problems** — difficulty falling or staying asleep
- **Weight gain** — especially around the midsection, even though nothing has changed
- **Mood swings** — irritable, anxious, or down without an obvious reason
- **Brain fog** — concentration problems and forgetfulness
- **Cycle changes** — shorter, longer, heavier, or irregular
- **Joint pain and stiffness** — especially in the morning
- **Libido changes** — less desire or altered sensations
