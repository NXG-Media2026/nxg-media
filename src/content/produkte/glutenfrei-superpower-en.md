## Gluten-free — done right

Going gluten-free doesn't mean deprivation. It also doesn't mean you'll only eat rice crackers and dry lettuce leaves from now on.

This mini guide shows you how to integrate gluten-free eating into your everyday life in a way that feels good — not like a diet, but like a conscious choice.

### Why this guide?

You've probably already googled it. And then: contradictory information, expensive specialty products, complicated recipes.

This guide is different:

- **Compact** — not a 200-page handbook, but what you actually need
- **Practical** — recipes with normal ingredients from the supermarket
- **Honest** — going gluten-free isn't necessary for every woman, and we say that too

### What you get

- **Gluten-free basics** — what gluten is, why it causes problems for some women, and when it makes sense to avoid it
- **15 simple recipes** — breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks the whole family will enjoy
- **Shopping checklist** — what you should always have at home
- **Label guide** — spot hidden gluten so you shop with confidence

### Your entry point for under €8

This guide costs less than a gluten-free loaf at the health food store. But it gives you the knowledge and recipes you need to make gluten-free eating simple and delicious.
