Coach Josine specializes in eating disorders and already had a loyal audience, but no digital products to turn that attention into scalable revenue.

## The challenge

Without a product, every client depended on direct coaching. There was no entry-level offer to qualify warm leads.

## What we built

We created the Eating Struggle Guide as a first mini-product and built a masterclass funnel with automated email follow-up for the launch.

## The result

In the first weeks: 600 guides sold and 80 masterclass signups — a solid foundation for further product expansion.
