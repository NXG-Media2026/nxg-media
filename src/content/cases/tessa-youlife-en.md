Tessa ran a successful coaching practice but had no online products, no email list, and no scalable system to attract new clients.

## The challenge

Relying on word-of-mouth and Instagram made her lead flow unpredictable. One month she would get three new clients, the next month none.

## What we built

We started with a single mini-product as a paid entry-level offer, added checkout bump offers, and scaled up with Meta Ads. Within four months the product line grew from 1 to 6 products.

## The result

From €0 to roughly €10,000 per month in online product revenue, with an average of 10 coaching clients per month coming in through product leads.
