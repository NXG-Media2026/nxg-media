Happy With Yoga is one of the largest yoga platforms in the Netherlands, founded by a yoga teacher with a passion for accessible yoga. The platform already attracted hundreds of thousands of visitors per month but lacked a system to turn that traffic into paying members.

## The challenge

The reach was already there — but conversion was missing. Visitors came for free content, yet there was no ladder guiding them toward paid products or membership. Revenue was unpredictable and dependent on one-off campaigns.

## What we built

We designed a complete growth system around three pillars: a quiz funnel for segmentation, a webinar structure as a warm introduction, and the Chakra mini-course as a paid entry-level product. Around that we built email flows and Meta Ads that continuously feed the system.

## The result

Over three years the platform grew to more than 30,000 webinar leads, 1,700+ mini-course sales, and 2,000 paying platform members — powered by a system that is predictable and scalable.
