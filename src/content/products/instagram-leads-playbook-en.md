## What most coaches try (and why it doesn't scale)

You probably recognise at least two of these:

- **Posting content endlessly** and hoping the algorithm picks you up. An occasional good reel, but no system behind it.
- **Sending cold DMs** to potential clients. Thousands per month for 1 or 2 clients. Feels pushy, isn't scalable, and risks your account getting restricted.
- **Running ads** without a strategy. Burning budget on campaigns that don't convert because there's no funnel behind them.
- **Doing everything at once** — podcasts, YouTube, LinkedIn, Instagram, newsletters. Lots of energy, no focus, no results.

The problem isn't your visibility. The problem is you don't have a **system** that turns that visibility into actual conversations.
