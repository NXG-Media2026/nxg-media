## What most business owners try (and why it doesn't work)

You've probably tried a few things already:

- **Posting more on social media** — but Instagram likes are invisible to ChatGPT
- **Getting SEO copy written** — but Google ranking and AI visibility are two different things
- **A nicer website** — but design doesn't matter if AI can't read and cite your content

The problem runs deeper. ChatGPT doesn't pick who's best. It picks who it **can prove**.

## Three layers — and you need all three

AI visibility works like a pyramid with three layers. Miss one, and AI misses the whole picture:

**Layer 1 — Foundation.** Can AI technically find and read your site? Most sites fail here already. Not because they're bad, but because they were never built with AI in mind.

**Layer 2 — Citability.** Can AI use your text as an answer to a question? "We help entrepreneurs grow" is too vague. "We help SMBs with €1,500+ budgets generate 15-30 leads/month via Google Ads" — that's something ChatGPT can relay.

**Layer 3 — Authority.** Does AI have a reason to choose you over others? Without reviews, cases and proof blocks, AI always picks the competitor who does have that proof.
