AI visibility is the extent to which your business or practice is recommended by AI tools such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini when someone searches for help in your area.

## Do you recognise this?

Your practice is doing well. You have satisfied clients, good reviews and a solid website. But when someone asks ChatGPT "Who is the best physiotherapist in Amersfoort?" or "Which skin therapist in Valencia is good?", you're not mentioned.

## What doesn't work

Doing classic SEO for an AI world. GBP optimisation without citable content. Hoping AI will find you on its own.

## The mechanism

AI tools use a different recommendation mechanism than Google Search. Not clicks, but citability, context and authority.
