## What it means to be in perimenopause

Perimenopause is not a disease — it's a natural life phase. But it brings changes that you can actively support.

### What's happening hormonally?

During perimenopause, estrogen production begins to fluctuate and gradually decline. At the same time, progesterone levels often drop faster. This imbalance is responsible for many of the typical symptoms.

### The three pillars of perimenopause health

1. **Strength training** — At least 2–3x per week. Essential for bone health, muscle mass, and metabolism.
2. **Protein-rich nutrition** — Your protein needs increase. Target: 1.2–1.6 g/kg body weight per day.
3. **Sleep & stress management** — Sleep hygiene and active relaxation are more important than ever in this phase.

### When to see a doctor?

If your symptoms are significantly affecting your daily life, you should talk to your doctor — including about possible hormone replacement therapy (HRT).
