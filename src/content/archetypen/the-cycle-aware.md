## What it means to be "Cycle-Aware"

You already have a solid foundation — now it's about fine-tuning. Cycle-aware living means using your natural hormonal fluctuations as a resource rather than seeing them as an obstacle.

### Using the four phases

Each cycle phase brings its own strengths:

- **Menstruation (Days 1–5)**: Time for reflection and light movement
- **Follicular phase (Days 6–13)**: Energy and creativity rise — ideal for new projects and intense training
- **Ovulation (Days 14–16)**: Your energy peak — use it for challenges
- **Luteal phase (Days 17–28)**: Focus on routine, detail work, and recovery

### Nutrition by phase

Adapt your nutrition to each phase's needs: more iron during menstruation, lighter foods in the follicular phase, antioxidants around ovulation, complex carbohydrates and magnesium in the luteal phase.
