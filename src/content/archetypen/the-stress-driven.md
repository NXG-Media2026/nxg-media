## What it means to be "Stress-Driven"

Stress isn't just a feeling — it's a measurable hormonal reaction. When your body is constantly in fight-or-flight mode, the production of stress hormones takes priority over all other hormonal processes.

### The cortisol-progesterone connection

Cortisol and progesterone are produced from the same precursor molecule (pregnenolone). During chronic stress, your body "steals" pregnenolone for cortisol production — at the expense of your progesterone. This is known as the "pregnenolone steal."

### Your path back to balance

1. **Prioritize sleep** — 7–9 hours, consistent bedtime
2. **Adjust training** — less HIIT, more moderate movement
3. **Stabilize nutrition** — regular meals, sufficient protein
4. **Active relaxation** — breathing exercises, meditation, walks in nature
