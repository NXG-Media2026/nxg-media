## What it means to be a "Power Athlete"

Performance and health don't have to be at odds — but when you ignore your body's signals, the scales can tip.

### Understanding RED-S

RED-S (Relative Energy Deficiency in Sport) is more than just "not eating enough." It describes a mismatch between energy intake and energy expenditure that affects all body systems. Your hormone system is particularly sensitive.

### Cycle-aware training as a solution

Instead of training against your hormones, you can work with them:

- **Follicular phase**: You're more capable here — ideal for intense training
- **Luteal phase**: Your body needs more recovery — moderate sessions are more effective
- **Menstruation**: Lighter movement like yoga or walks

### Nutrition for athletes

Sufficient energy is the foundation. Especially important: protein (1.6–2.2 g/kg), iron, calcium, vitamin D, and omega-3 fatty acids.
